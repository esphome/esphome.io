The `tlv320dac3100` platform allows your ESPHome devices to use the TI TLV320DAC3100 stereo audio DAC, which has a
built-in mono class-D speaker amplifier and a stereo headphone driver. This allows the playback of audio via the
microcontroller from a range of sources via [Media Player](/components/media_player/).

The [I²C bus](/components/i2c) is required in your configuration as this is used to communicate with the
TLV320DAC3100. Audio data comes from an [I²S Audio Speaker](/components/speaker/i2s_audio/).

```yaml
# Example configuration entry
audio_dac:
  - platform: tlv320dac3100
    id: dac
    sample_rate: 44100

speaker:
  - platform: i2s_audio
    dac_type: external
    i2s_dout_pin: GPIOXX
    sample_rate: 44100
    audio_dac: dac
```

## Configuration variables

- **sample_rate** (**Required**, int): The sample rate of the audio sent to the DAC, from `8000` to `192000`. Must
  match the `sample_rate` of the I²S speaker.
- **bits_per_sample** (*Optional*, string): The I²S sample size: `16bit`, `24bit` or `32bit`. Must match the I²S
  speaker. Defaults to `16bit`.
- **mclk_frequency** (*Optional*, int): The frequency of the clock on the MCLK pin, in Hz, up to 50 MHz. Without it,
  the DAC's clocks are generated from the I²S bit clock (BCLK), so no MCLK connection is needed.
  When the I²S bus drives `i2s_mclk_pin` from a primary-mode speaker, this must equal the speaker's `sample_rate`
  multiplied by its `mclk_multiple`.
- **output** (*Optional*, string): The outputs to enable: `SPEAKER`, `HEADPHONE` or `BOTH`. With `SPEAKER`, the
  left and right channels are mixed for the mono speaker. Defaults to `BOTH`.
- **speaker_gain** (*Optional*, string): The class-D speaker amplifier gain: `6dB`, `12dB`, `18dB` or `24dB`.
  Defaults to `6dB`.
- **reset_pin** (*Optional*, [Pin Schema](/guides/configuration-types/#pin-schema)): The pin connected to the DAC's
  RESET input. Boards that leave RESET to the microcontroller, such as the Adafruit breakout, need this.
- **address** (*Optional*, int): The I²C address of the driver. Defaults to `0x18`.
- **i2c_id** (*Optional*): The ID of the [I²C bus](/components/i2c) the TLV320DAC3100 is connected to.
- All other options from [Audio DAC](/components/audio_dac/#config-audio_dac).

> [!NOTE]
> The clock dividers are calculated from `sample_rate` and the clock source when the configuration is validated. A
> combination the DAC can't produce within its datasheet limits is rejected. For example, 8 kHz with 16-bit samples
> gives a BCLK below the PLL's minimum input; use `32bit` instead.

> [!NOTE]
> The DAC needs its clock running while it powers up, so it powers up when audio first plays. The outputs stay muted
> until about 0.1 s after that, which keeps the speaker free of pops. An I²S speaker must name this DAC in its
> `audio_dac` option; a speaker further up the chain, such as a mixer, does not report when the clocks start.
> That speaker must use `dac_type: external` and standard I²S (the default `i2s_comm_fmt`), not S/PDIF.

> [!NOTE]
> The clock dividers are fixed for `sample_rate`, so audio has to reach the I²S speaker at that rate. Use a
> [Resampler Speaker](/components/speaker/resampler/) or set the media player pipeline's `sample_rate`.
