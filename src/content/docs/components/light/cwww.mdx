The `cwww` light platform creates a cold white + warm white light from 2
[float output components](/components/output/) (one for each channel). The two channels
can be controlled individually or together.

```yaml
# Example configuration entry
light:
  - platform: cwww
    name: "Livingroom Lights"
    cold_white: output_component1
    warm_white: output_component2
    cold_white_color_temperature: 6536 K
    warm_white_color_temperature: 2000 K
    constant_brightness: true
```

## Mixing

The two channels of this light can be controlled individually by using the `cold_white` and `warm_white` options of
the [light control actions](/components/light/#light-turn_on-action).

If the color temperature of both lights is supplied, it is also possible to control the two channels together by
setting a color temperature, using the `white` (interpreted as brightness) and `color_temperature` options. This
calculation assumes that both lights have the same illuminance, which might not always be accurate. If they differ,
set [`cold_to_warm_ratio`](#cold-to-warm-ratio).

## Constant Brightness

The setting decides what the two channels do at 100% brightness as the color temperature changes.

| `constant_brightness` | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `false` | Each channel follows its share of the color temperature. Both channels are full in the middle of the range, so the light is brightest there. |
| `true` | The total output stays constant, at most one channel's full output. This reduces the maximum brightness, but suits power supplies that cannot run both channels at full brightness at once. |
| `color_temperature_priority` | The light stays constant across color temperatures while both channels have headroom. Beyond that point the color temperature wins and the output drops toward the ends of the range. 100% means both channels are full where they balance, so the power supply must be able to run both at full output. |

```yaml
light:
  - platform: cwww
    name: "Livingroom Lights"
    cold_white: output_component1
    warm_white: output_component2
    cold_white_color_temperature: 6536 K
    warm_white_color_temperature: 2000 K
    constant_brightness: color_temperature_priority
```

> [!NOTE]
> With `color_temperature_priority`, "constant" refers to linear light output. With the default
> [`gamma_correct`](/components/light/#config-light) of `2.8` and equal channels, the constant range reaches about 78%
> on the brightness slider. With a [`cold_to_warm_ratio`](#cold-to-warm-ratio) other than `1`, it ends earlier: the
> weaker channel alone has to be able to give the light.

## Cold to Warm Ratio

Cold and warm white strips rarely give the same amount of light at full output. `cold_to_warm_ratio` is the light of
the cold channel divided by the light of the warm channel, both at full output. With the ratio set, the channels are
mixed by light instead of by output level, so the color temperature shown is the real mix. This works with every
`constant_brightness` setting.

To get the ratio, divide the measured lumens of the cold strip by those of the warm strip. If both strips have a similar
efficiency, you can divide their current (A) or power (W) instead. For example, a cold strip drawing 0.92 A and a warm
strip drawing 1.30 A gives `0.92 / 1.30 = 0.708`.

```yaml
light:
  - platform: cwww
    name: "Livingroom Lights"
    cold_white: output_component1
    warm_white: output_component2
    cold_white_color_temperature: 6536 K
    warm_white_color_temperature: 2000 K
    cold_to_warm_ratio: 0.708
```

> [!NOTE]
> Setting `max_power` on the stronger [output](/components/output/) also balances the channels, but that strip's extra
> output is then not used. `cold_to_warm_ratio` keeps it.

## Configuration variables

- **cold_white** (**Required**, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The id of the float [Output Component](/components/output/) to use for the cold white channel.
- **warm_white** (**Required**, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The id of the float [Output Component](/components/output/) to use for the warm white channel.
- **cold_white_color_temperature** (*Optional*, float): The color temperature (in [mireds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mired) or Kelvin)
  of the cold white channel. Note that this option is required to control the mixing from Home Assistant.

- **warm_white_color_temperature** (*Optional*, float): The color temperature (in [mireds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mired) or Kelvin)
  of the warm white channel. Note that this option is required to control the mixing from Home Assistant.

- **constant_brightness** (*Optional*, boolean or `color_temperature_priority`): How the brightness is spread over the
  two channels. Defaults to `false`. See [Constant Brightness](#constant-brightness).
- **cold_to_warm_ratio** (*Optional*, float): The light of the cold white channel divided by the light of the warm white
  channel, both at full output. Range `0.01` to `100`. Defaults to `1.0`. See [Cold to Warm Ratio](#cold-to-warm-ratio).
- All other options from [Light](/components/light/#config-light).
