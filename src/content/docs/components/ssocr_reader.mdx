The `ssocr_reader` component reads a seven-segment display, such as the counter of an energy or water meter, out of
frames from an [ESP32 Camera](/components/esp32_camera/). It decodes each frame once, crops the configured rectangle
out of that image and reads the digits with `seven_segment_ocr`. No trained model is involved: the crop is thresholded
and every digit is recognised from its seven segments, so a clean, tightly cropped and evenly lit display is needed.

<Image src="/images/7_segment_display.png" width={350} height={350} layout="constrained" alt="A seven-segment display with the segments labelled A to G and the decimal point DP" />

A seven-segment display draws each digit from the seven segments labelled A to G.

```yaml
camera_decoder:
  id: meter_decoder
  output_type: GRAYSCALE

ssocr_reader:
  camera_id: my_camera
  decoder_id: meter_decoder
  crop_x: 100
  crop_y: 200
  crop_w: 300
  crop_h: 80
  digit_count: 6
  value:
    name: "Water meter"
  confidence:
    name: "Water meter confidence"
```

## Configuration variables

- **id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The reader ID.
- **camera_id** (**Required**, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The `esp32_camera` that produces the frames.
- **decoder_id** (**Required**, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The `camera_decoder` that decodes the frames.
  `output_type: GRAYSCALE` uses the least RAM; `RGB888` also works and is converted to grayscale.
- **crop_x** (*Optional*, int): Left edge of the crop in the decoded frame. Defaults to `0`.
- **crop_y** (*Optional*, int): Top edge of the crop. Defaults to `0`.
- **crop_w** (**Required**, int): Width of the rectangle around the digits.
- **crop_h** (**Required**, int): Height of the rectangle around the digits.
- **threshold_level** (*Optional*, int): Pixels above this level, from 0 to 255, count as lit. Defaults to `128`.
- **digit_count** (*Optional*, int): How many digits to read, from 1 to 16. Defaults to `6`.
- **value** (*Optional*, [Sensor](/components/sensor/)): The reading.
- **confidence** (*Optional*, [Sensor](/components/sensor/)): How confident the recognition is, from 0 to 100 %.
- **debug** (*Optional*, boolean): Log the update cycle and each reading. Defaults to `false`.
- **update_interval** (*Optional*, [Time](/guides/configuration-types/#time)): How often to request a frame.
  Defaults to `60s`.

## How a display is read

1. The camera frame is decoded once.
2. The configured rectangle is copied out as one byte per pixel. An `RGB888` decoder is converted to luma.
3. `seven_segment_ocr` thresholds the crop, groups the lit columns into digit runs and samples the seven segments of
   each run.
4. When exactly `digit_count` digits were found and all of them were recognised, `value` and `confidence` are
   published.

A digit run narrower than a third of the image height is a single bar and is read as a `1`.

## Requirements and behaviour

- The camera must deliver JPEG frames. Use `pixel_format: JPEG`, or set `jpeg_quality` on a camera with another pixel
  format so the frames are converted to JPEG for the reader.
- The crop must contain only the digits and must be aligned with them: the reader does not rotate, deskew or scale.
- The digits must be bright on a dark background. If the display is dark on a light background, the reader cannot
  read it.
- The digits must sit in one row and have the same size.
- When a digit is missing or unknown, nothing is published and a warning reports how many digits were found.
  `debug: true` adds each reading to the log.
- The frame size is taken from the camera's `resolution`. A `camera_window` that makes the frames smaller is
  supported as long as the crop still fits.
