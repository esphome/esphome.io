The `npm1300` component configures and monitors the Nordic Semiconductor nPM1300 power management IC (PMIC) over
the [I²C bus](/components/i2c/) ([product specification](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/bundle/ps_npm1300/page/keyfeatures_html5.html)).
The nPM1300 has a 1-cell lithium-ion battery charger, two buck regulators (BUCK1, BUCK2), two LDOs or load switches
(LDO1, LDO2) and a 10-bit ADC that measures the battery, the system supply and the USB supply (VBUS).

The hub writes the VBUS current limit, the charger and the regulator settings at boot and reads the chip at
`update_interval`. The `sensor`, `binary_sensor` and `text_sensor` platforms report measurements and the charger
state, and the `switch` platform turns the charger and the regulators on and off.

## Configuration

```yaml
# Example configuration entry
npm1300:
  id: pmic
  vbus_current_limit: 500mA
  charger:
    charge_current: 150mA
    termination_voltage: 4.2V

sensor:
  - platform: npm1300
    battery_voltage:
      name: "Battery Voltage"
    battery_current:
      name: "Battery Current"

binary_sensor:
  - platform: npm1300
    charging:
      name: "Charging"

text_sensor:
  - platform: npm1300
    charger_status:
      name: "Charger Status"
```

## Configuration Variables

- **id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): Manually specify the ID used to reference this hub
  from the platforms and actions.
- **address** (*Optional*, int): The I²C address of the chip. Defaults to `0x6B`.
- **i2c_id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): Manually specify the ID of the I²C bus.
- **update_interval** (*Optional*, [Time](/guides/configuration-types/#time)): How often to read the chip.
  Defaults to `60s`.
- **vbus_current_limit** (*Optional*, current): The current the chip may draw from VBUS (USB).
  `100mA` to `1500mA` in 100 mA steps. The chip starts with `100mA` and returns to it every time VBUS is removed, so
  the limit is written again on every update while VBUS is present. If not set, the chip default is kept.
- **thermistor_resistance** (*Optional*, resistance): The nominal resistance of the battery thermistor (NTC), one of
  `10kOhm`, `47kOhm` or `100kOhm`. Needed for the `battery_temperature` sensor. If the `charger` is configured
  without a thermistor, the charger does not check the battery temperature.
- **thermistor_beta** (*Optional*, int): The B constant of the battery thermistor. Defaults to `3380`.
- **charger** (*Optional*): Configure and turn on the battery charger. See [Charger](#charger).
- **buck1**, **buck2** (*Optional*): Configure a buck regulator. See [Regulators](#regulators).
- **ldo1**, **ldo2** (*Optional*): Configure an LDO or load switch. See [Regulators](#regulators).

The settings are written in this order: VBUS current limit, thermistor, charger, then the regulators. Raising the
VBUS current limit first keeps the inrush current of a regulator from tripping the over-current protection of the
chip when the board runs from USB.

### Charger

- **charge_current** (**Required**, current): The charge current, from `32mA` to `800mA` in 2 mA steps.
- **termination_voltage** (*Optional*, voltage): The voltage at which charging ends. `3.5V` to `3.65V` or `4.0V` to
  `4.45V`, in 50 mV steps. Defaults to `4.2V`.
- **warm_termination_voltage** (*Optional*, voltage): The termination voltage when the battery is warm, with the same
  values as `termination_voltage`. Defaults to `termination_voltage`.
- **termination_current** (*Optional*, string): The charge current, as a share of `charge_current`, below which
  charging is complete. One of `10%` or `20%`. Defaults to `10%`.
- **trickle_voltage** (*Optional*, voltage): Below this battery voltage the battery is charged with a small trickle
  current. One of `2.5V` or `2.9V`. Defaults to `2.9V`.
- **discharge_current_limit** (*Optional*, current): The battery discharge current limit. One of `200mA` or `1000mA`.
  Defaults to `1000mA`.
- **recharge** (*Optional*, boolean): Start charging again when the battery voltage drops after a complete charge.
  Defaults to `true`.
- **enabled** (*Optional*, boolean): Turn on the charger at boot. Defaults to `true`.

> [!WARNING]
> Check `charge_current` and the termination voltages against the data sheet of your battery. Wrong values can damage
> the battery.

### Regulators

- **voltage** (*Optional*, voltage): The output voltage, from `1.0V` to `3.3V` in 100 mV steps. For a buck
  regulator this replaces the voltage set by the `VSET` pin of the chip. For an LDO, it applies in `ldo` mode.
- **mode** (*Optional*, string): LDO1 and LDO2 only: `ldo` to regulate the output voltage, or `load_switch` to pass
  the input voltage through.
- **enabled** (*Optional*, boolean): Turn the output on (`true`) or off (`false`) at boot. If not set, the output
  stays as it is.

> [!CAUTION]
> One of the buck regulators usually powers the microcontroller itself. Turning it off, or setting a voltage the
> microcontroller does not support, turns off or damages the board.

## Sensor

```yaml
sensor:
  - platform: npm1300
    battery_voltage:
      name: "Battery Voltage"
    battery_current:
      name: "Battery Current"
    battery_temperature:
      name: "Battery Temperature"
    die_temperature:
      name: "PMIC Temperature"
    system_voltage:
      name: "System Voltage"
    vbus_voltage:
      name: "VBUS Voltage"
```

- **npm1300_id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The ID of the `npm1300` hub.
- **battery_voltage** (*Optional*): The battery voltage, in volts.
- **battery_current** (*Optional*): The battery current, in amperes: positive while charging, negative while
  discharging. The chip measures it relative to the charge current or the discharge current limit, so it is most
  accurate near those values.
- **battery_temperature** (*Optional*): The battery temperature from the thermistor, in °C. Needs
  `thermistor_resistance` on the hub.
- **die_temperature** (*Optional*): The temperature of the nPM1300 itself, in °C.
- **system_voltage** (*Optional*): The system supply voltage (VSYS), in volts.
- **vbus_voltage** (*Optional*): The USB supply voltage (VBUS), in volts.
- **vbus_current_limit** (*Optional*): The VBUS current limit stored in the chip, in amperes. The chip cannot
  measure the VBUS current itself. This is the value software selected, so it is only accurate with
  `vbus_current_limit` set on the hub, which applies it on every update. Without it, the sensor shows the reset
  value of the register (0.5 A) while the chip uses its power-on limit of 100 mA.

All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor/).

## Binary Sensor

```yaml
binary_sensor:
  - platform: npm1300
    vbus_present:
      name: "USB Power"
    charging:
      name: "Charging"
```

- **npm1300_id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The ID of the `npm1300` hub.
- **vbus_present** (*Optional*): On when a supply is connected to VBUS.
- **vbus_current_limit_active** (*Optional*): On while the VBUS current is held at its limit. The battery then
  supplies the rest of the load, or charging slows down. If this is on often, raise `vbus_current_limit`.
- **charging** (*Optional*): On while the battery is charging (trickle, constant current or constant voltage).
- **charge_complete** (*Optional*): On when charging is complete.
- **battery_detected** (*Optional*): On when the charger detects a battery.
- **charger_error** (*Optional*): On when the charger stopped because of an error, for example a battery or
  thermistor fault or a charge timeout. Turn the charger switch off and on to clear the error.

All options from [Binary Sensor](/components/binary_sensor/).

## Text Sensor

```yaml
text_sensor:
  - platform: npm1300
    charger_status:
      name: "Charger Status"
```

- **npm1300_id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The ID of the `npm1300` hub.
- **charger_status** (*Optional*): The state of the charger: `Error`, `Paused, Die Too Hot`, `Charge Complete`,
  `Trickle Charge`, `Constant Current`, `Constant Voltage`, `Discharging` (no VBUS), `No Battery` or
  `Not Charging`.

All options from [Text Sensor](/components/text_sensor/).

## Switch

```yaml
switch:
  - platform: npm1300
    charger:
      name: "Charger"
    ldo1:
      name: "LDO1"
```

- **npm1300_id** (*Optional*, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The ID of the `npm1300` hub.
- **charger** (*Optional*): Turn the battery charger on or off. Turning it on also clears a charger error.
- **buck1**, **buck2**, **ldo1**, **ldo2** (*Optional*): Turn a regulator on or off.

The switches show the state read from the chip at every update, so their state is not restored at boot.

All options from [Switch](/components/switch/).

## `npm1300.enter_ship_mode` Action

Puts the chip in ship mode, its lowest power state: all outputs are turned off and the device stays off until the
`SHPHLD` button of the nPM1300 is pressed or VBUS is connected.

```yaml
on_...:
  then:
    - npm1300.enter_ship_mode: pmic
```

## `npm1300.hibernate` Action

Puts the chip in hibernate mode: all outputs are turned off until the wake-up timer of the chip runs out, the
`SHPHLD` button is pressed or VBUS is connected. The device then starts again from the beginning.

```yaml
on_...:
  then:
    - npm1300.hibernate:
        id: pmic
        duration: 1h
```

- **id** (**Required**, [ID](/guides/configuration-types/#id)): The ID of the `npm1300` hub.
- **duration** (**Required**, [templatable](/automations/templates/), [Time](/guides/configuration-types/#time)): How
  long to stay in hibernate mode, from `16ms` to about `74h`, in 16 ms steps.

> [!NOTE]
> A supply on VBUS wakes the chip, so with VBUS connected it leaves ship or hibernate mode right away.

## Seeed Studio XIAO nRF54LM20A

The XIAO nRF54LM20A boards have an nPM1300 at address `0x6B` on its own I²C bus on `P1.18` (SDA) and `P1.17` (SCL),
with a 10 kΩ battery thermistor. BUCK2 powers the nRF54LM20A and must stay on, and LDO1 supplies 3.3 V to the IMU
and the microphone of the Sense version. This configuration matches the settings of the board in Zephyr:

```yaml
i2c:
  - id: pmic_bus
    sda: P1.18
    scl: P1.17

npm1300:
  id: pmic
  i2c_id: pmic_bus
  vbus_current_limit: 500mA
  thermistor_resistance: 10kOhm
  thermistor_beta: 3380
  charger:
    charge_current: 150mA
    termination_voltage: 4.2V
    warm_termination_voltage: 4.0V
  ldo1:
    voltage: 3.3V
    mode: ldo
    enabled: true

sensor:
  - platform: npm1300
    battery_voltage:
      name: "Battery Voltage"
    battery_current:
      name: "Battery Current"
    battery_temperature:
      name: "Battery Temperature"

text_sensor:
  - platform: npm1300
    charger_status:
      name: "Charger Status"
```
