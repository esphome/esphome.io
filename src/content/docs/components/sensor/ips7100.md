The `ips7100` sensor platform allows you to use your Piera Systems IPS-7100
Intelligent Particle Sensor
([datasheet](https://pierasystems.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/IPS-Datasheet-V1.3.8.pdf),
[Piera Systems](https://pierasystems.com/product/piera-7100-intelligent-particle-sensor/)) with ESPHome.
The sensor uses laser scattering to measure both mass concentration (µg/m³) and particle count (#/cm³)
across 7 size bins: 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 1.0, 2.5, 5.0, and 10.0 µm.

The [I²C Bus](/components/i2c) is required to be set up in your configuration for this sensor to work.

{{< img src="ips7100.jpg" alt="IPS-7100 Particulate Matter Sensor" width="50.0%" class="align-center" >}}

> [!NOTE]
> The SEL pin must be connected to GND to enable I²C mode.

```yaml
# Example configuration entry
sensor:
  - platform: ips7100
    pm_1_0:
      name: "PM <1.0µm"
    pm_2_5:
      name: "PM <2.5µm"
    pm_10_0:
      name: "PM <10.0µm"
    pmc_0_5:
      name: "PMC 0.5"
    pmc_1_0:
      name: "PMC 1.0"
    pmc_2_5:
      name: "PMC 2.5"
```

## Configuration variables

- **pm_0_1** (*Optional*): Mass of particles with a diameter of 0.1 micrometres or less (μg/m³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pm_0_3** (*Optional*): Mass of particles with a diameter of 0.3 micrometres or less (μg/m³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pm_0_5** (*Optional*): Mass of particles with a diameter of 0.5 micrometres or less (μg/m³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pm_1_0** (*Optional*): Mass of particles with a diameter of 1.0 micrometres or less (μg/m³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pm_2_5** (*Optional*): Mass of particles with a diameter of 2.5 micrometres or less (μg/m³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pm_5_0** (*Optional*): Mass of particles with a diameter of 5.0 micrometres or less (μg/m³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pm_10_0** (*Optional*): Mass of particles with a diameter of 10.0 micrometres or less (μg/m³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pmc_0_1** (*Optional*): Count of particles with diameter > 0.1 µm (#/cm³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pmc_0_3** (*Optional*): Count of particles with diameter > 0.3 µm (#/cm³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pmc_0_5** (*Optional*): Count of particles with diameter > 0.5 µm (#/cm³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pmc_1_0** (*Optional*): Count of particles with diameter > 1.0 µm (#/cm³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pmc_2_5** (*Optional*): Count of particles with diameter > 2.5 µm (#/cm³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pmc_5_0** (*Optional*): Count of particles with diameter > 5.0 µm (#/cm³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **pmc_10_0** (*Optional*): Count of particles with diameter > 10.0 µm (#/cm³).
  All options from [Sensor](/components/sensor).

- **address** (*Optional*, int): Manually specify the I²C address of the sensor.
  Defaults to `0x4B`.

- **update_interval** (*Optional*, [Time](/guides/configuration-types#time)): The interval to check the
  sensor. Defaults to `60s`.
